## Where to hunt for the still-missing writeups

No per-service **A/D** writeup for any D-CTF final was found in this research. DefCamp has offered a **best-writeup prize** (top-30 teams) since 2023, so they likely exist. Best places to look:

1. **Finalist team blogs/repos** — TRX, OctalO, Hackemus Papam, PTB_WTL_0T, Lucky Lucian, The Few Chosen, Wreck the Line.
2. **CyberEDU** service descriptions (the 2022 final was hosted there).
3. **CTFtime event pages' Writeups tab** — [2022](https://ctftime.org/event/1824), [2023](https://ctftime.org/event/2182), plus the 2024/2025 finals.
4. **DefCamp blog** ([def.camp](https://def.camp/)) wrap-ups and walkthroughs.
5. Aggregators: [CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/), [sajjadium/ctf-archives](https://github.com/sajjadium/ctf-archives) (challenge files; currently **quals only** for DefCamp), [ctfs/write-ups-*](https://github.com/ctfs).

> The cloud environment used for this research could not reach `ctftime.org`, `def.camp`, or most team blogs directly (only GitHub + web-search excerpts). To close the gaps, allow those hosts in the environment's network settings and re-run.
